Jeonghwan Kim is an Integrated M.S. & Ph.D. student at DGIST, advised by Prof. Jeongho Kwak (DGIST / Korea University) in the Intelligent Computing & Networking Laboratory. His research focuses on intelligent LEO satellite networks and onboard edge computing, with an emphasis on joint communication, computation, and routing optimization for battery-aware resource management and connected autonomous vehicles.

  • Integrated M.S. & Ph.D. Program, Electrical Engineering & Computer Science, DGIST. Advisor: Prof. Jeongho Kwak (DGIST / Korea University). March 2022 – Present.
